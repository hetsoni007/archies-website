The Studio

A label built on craft & care

Founded by Archana Soni in Ahmedabad, Aarchi's crafts timeless designs that celebrate elegance and individuality — from bridal lehengas with zari, zardozi and khat work to handcrafted sarees and festive wear.

Every piece is made to measure and designed around you, blending traditional craftsmanship with a contemporary sensibility.
